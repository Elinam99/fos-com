Fos Founders

Geoffrey and the Fos.com team are building a marketplace to make buying and selling fashion simple for everyone in Ghana.

About

Fos.com was created with a clear vision: connect buyers and sellers through a modern, trustworthy online marketplace. From curated summer collections to everyday wardrobe essentials, the platform is designed to feel local, fast, and easy to use.

As founders, the focus has been on clean design, fair pricing, and a shopping experience that works on both phone and desktop — so customers can browse, search, and order with confidence.

Vision

To grow Fos.com into Ghana's go-to destination for fashion — where small sellers can reach new customers and shoppers discover quality styles at great prices, with reliable delivery and customer support.
