Geoffrey leads Fos.com — building a marketplace to make buying and selling fashion simple for everyone in Ghana.

About Geoffrey

Fos.com was created with a clear vision: connect buyers and sellers through a modern, trustworthy online marketplace. From curated summer collections to everyday wardrobe essentials, the platform is designed to feel local, fast, and easy to use.

As co-founder, Geoffrey focuses on clean design, fair pricing, and a shopping experience that works on both phone and desktop — so customers can browse, search, and order with confidence.
