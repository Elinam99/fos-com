About Fos.com

Ghana's modern marketplace for buying and selling fashion — built for everyday shoppers and local sellers.

Who we are

Fos.com is an online fashion marketplace designed to make shopping simple, fast, and trustworthy. We bring together quality clothing, shoes, and accessories in one place so customers can discover new styles without the hassle.

From summer collections to daily wardrobe essentials, our platform is built with a clean experience on both mobile and desktop — search, browse deals, and order with confidence.

Our mission

To connect buyers and sellers across Ghana through a platform that feels local, fair, and easy to use — helping small businesses grow while giving shoppers access to great fashion at competitive prices.
